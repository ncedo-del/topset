What is TOPSET?

TOPSET is a performance and athletic club platform turning your workout data into a clear, traceable baseline — helping athletes and everyday enthusiasts build consistency, track progress, and compete on a single platform.

Through live scoreboards and traceable metrics, we take the guesswork out of training and turn individual effort into a shared community standard. Whether you're chasing your first major personal record or pushing to top a leaderboard, we give your hard work the proof it deserves.

Traditional, pure, and hybrid sports all considered.

Add TOPSET to Your Home Screen

This app can be saved among your apps — no App Store needed. Opens full-screen, loads faster, and keeps your streak one tap away.

Android — Chrome

  1. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top-right corner
  2. Tap Add to Home screen
  3. Tap Add to confirm

iPhone — Safari

  1. Tap the Share icon (square with an arrow) in the bottom bar
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
  3. Tap Add in the top-right corner
